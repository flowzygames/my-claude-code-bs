Rules of the house

  • Granny hears everything. Slamming doors, sprinting, smashing crates and gunshots pull her straight to you.
  • If she catches you, you lose a day. On day 5 there is no waking up.
  • Anything you unlock, unscrew or smash stays that way between days. Progress is permanent — only you get reset.
  • Hide in wardrobes and under beds. She will walk right past you if she didn't see you climb in.
  • A tranquilliser dart buys you twenty seconds. Bear traps buy you more, if you can place one in her path.
  • There are two ways out. One through the front door, one under the house.
